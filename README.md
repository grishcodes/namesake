# Namesake

**Create your own Jarvis.** A voice assistant that lives on your Windows laptop, answers to the name you give it, and can do anything you do with a keyboard and trackpad. Private by default. Free.

**Website:** https://namesake-woad.vercel.app

## Download

Get **Namesake-Setup.exe** from the [latest release](https://github.com/grishcodes/namesake/releases/latest) (Windows 10 and 11, about 190 MB).

1. Open the file. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. That appears for new apps that aren't signed yet.
2. Click **Install**. No admin password needed; it installs just for you.
3. Start talking. Your new assistant opens and introduces itself.

Installed copies check this page once a day and ask before installing an update.
